## Design Context

### Users
Indonesian Gen Z music creators — bedroom producers, aspiring artists, content creators who make music for TikTok, Reels, and YouTube. They're 17–28, music-obsessed, mobile-native, and discover Kreasi AI through social media. They want something that feels as electric and bold as the music they make — not another generic "AI tool."

### Brand Personality
**Bold, electric, alive.**

Three-word profile: **Passionate · Raw · Gen Z**

Tone: Like a music producer friend who's plugged into the underground scene. Direct, energetic, speaks in casual Bahasa Indonesia. Uses words like "gas!", "gokil", "vibe", "nendang". Never corporate. Always fires up the room.

Emotional goals: "Gila ini keren banget" on first visit → excitement to immediately try it → pride when the music comes out.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Dark Glassmorphism × Red Fire Energy**

Base is the same dark glass foundation as jiwa-ai (`#07070f`), but the accent system is built around **red as the dominant brand color** — not violet. Think: TikTok's electric red × underground Jakarta music scene × premium dark glass.

The red palette runs from deep crimson (`#dc2626`) through electric red (`#ff2d55`) to warm coral (`#ff6b6b`) — a fire gradient that reads as passionate and energetic. Paired with amber/orange as the warm complement and green for success states.

Think: Spotify × TikTok × Korean music app. Dark, glassy, cool — but lit with red fire instead of purple fog.

**Keep:**
- Dark background (`#07070f` — the void)
- Glassmorphism cards with backdrop blur
- Depth through layered translucent surfaces
- Syne for display headings (Gen Z bold)
- DM Sans for body text

**Evolve:**
- **Primary accent**: Red (`#ff2d55`) everywhere violet was — buttons, progress bars, active states, glows
- **Gradient story**: Fire gradient (crimson → red → coral → orange) replaces violet brand gradient
- **Cards**: Color-varied borders — red for music, amber for credits, coral for secondary actions
- **Typography**: Bigger, bolder, more expressive section headers — make Syne work hard
- **Background**: Red/crimson orbs floating in the void instead of violet fog
- **Waveform**: Red → orange → amber spectrum (fire frequency visualization)
- **Animations**: Springy, quick — not slow cinematic. Music is fast, the UI should match

### Color Palette

**Primary — Fire Red**
- `--accent-red: #ff2d55` — electric red, main brand CTA
- `--accent-crimson: #dc2626` — deep red, gradient depth
- `--accent-coral: #ff6b6b` — coral, secondary / softer red
- `--accent-rose: #f43f5e` — rose, hover states

**Warm Complement**
- `--accent-orange: #fb923c` — warm orange, creative energy
- `--accent-amber: #f59e0b` — amber, credits / premium / warnings

**Semantic**
- `--accent-green: #22c55e` — success, completed, downloaded
- `--accent-cyan: #22d3ee` — info, neutral-cool contrast

### Design Principles
1. **Red is the pulse** — primary actions, active states, brand moments are always red
2. **Fire gradient tells the story** — crimson → red → coral → orange flows like a waveform
3. **Glass with intention** — not every container gets glass; it marks premium/interactive surfaces
4. **Gen Z bold typography** — Syne at 800 weight for heroes, confident sizing, tight line-height
5. **Sound-native UI** — waveforms, frequency bars, audio player elements feel native to music culture
6. **Springy motion** — cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1) for reveals, not slow fades

### What to AVOID
- Violet/purple as primary — this is Kreasi, not Jiwa
- Gradient text on every heading — use it sparingly for hero moments only
- Generic rounded icon boxes above every section heading
- Identical card grids — vary size, weight, proportion
- Static aurora blobs as only background interest
- Cyan-dominant color stories — it's a secondary contrast color only
- Pure black (#000) or pure white (#fff)
- Slow cinematic fade animations on interactive elements
